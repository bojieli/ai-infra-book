Display Support
M6
Supports up to three external displays using Thunderbolt and HDMI ports:
Three displays: Two displays up to a native resolution of 6K at 60Hz or 4K at 165Hz, plus a third display up to 5K at 60Hz over Thunderbolt or 4K at 60Hz over HDMI
Up to two displays: One display up to a native resolution of 8K at 60Hz or 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 240Hz, plus a second display up to 5K at 60Hz over Thunderbolt or 4K at 60Hz over HDMI
Supports up to two external displays over a single Thunderbolt port
Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C) ports support native DisplayPort 1.4 speeds
Supports variable refresh rate (VRR), HDR, and multichannel audio
M5 Pro
Supports up to three external displays over any combination of Thunderbolt and HDMI ports:
Three displays up to a native resolution of 6K at 60Hz or 4K at 165Hz
Up to two displays: One display up to a native resolution of 8K at 60Hz or 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 240Hz, plus a second display up to 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 200Hz
Supports up to three external displays over a single Thunderbolt port
Thunderbolt 5 (USB-C) ports support native DisplayPort 2.1 speeds
Supports variable refresh rate (VRR), HDR, and multichannel audio
Learn more about connecting a display to your Mac

Progress toward Apple 2030
Apple 2030 is our goal to be carbon neutral across our value chain. We design our products to be less carbon intensive by prioritizing the use of recycled and renewable content and low-carbon materials while focusing on the energy efficiency of our software and hardware.
See Apple’s commitment
Materials
Mac mini is made with 50% recycled content,9 including:
100% recycled aluminum in the enclosure and thermal module stage
100% recycled copper in multiple thermal module components
100% recycled copper plating in all Apple-designed printed circuit boards
100% recycled gold plating and tin solder in all Apple-designed printed circuit boards
80% recycled steel in multiple components including the logo and fan pillow
Packaging
100% fiber‑based packaging10
Energy
100% of manufacturing electricity for Mac mini is sourced from renewable electricity11
100% of expected customer use electricity matched with low‑carbon electricity12
ENERGY STAR® certified13
Smarter chemistry14
All of the materials used in Apple products, accessories, and packaging are covered by the requirements of our Regulated Substances Specification, which was one of the first in industry to restrict the use of key substances of concern.
