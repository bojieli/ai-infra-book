Display Support
M5 Max
Supports up to five external displays over any combination of Thunderbolt and HDMI ports:
Five displays: Four displays up to a native resolution of 6K at 60Hz or 4K at 165Hz, plus a fifth display with up to 5K at 60Hz over HDMI
Up to three displays: Two displays up to a native resolution of 8K at 60Hz or 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 240Hz, plus a third display with up to 5K at 60Hz over HDMI
Supports up to four external displays over a single Thunderbolt port
Thunderbolt 5 (USB-C) ports support native DisplayPort 2.1 speeds
Supports variable refresh rate (VRR), HDR, and multichannel audio
M5 Ultra
Supports up to eight external displays over any combination of Thunderbolt and HDMI ports:
Eight displays up to a native resolution of 6K at 60Hz or 4K resolution at up to 165Hz
Up to four displays up to a native resolution of 8K at 60Hz or 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 240Hz
Supports up to four external displays over a single Thunderbolt port
Thunderbolt 5 (USB-C) ports support native DisplayPort 2.1 speeds
Supports variable refresh rate (VRR), HDR, and multichannel audio
