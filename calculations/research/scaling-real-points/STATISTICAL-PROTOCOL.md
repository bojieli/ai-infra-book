# Datablations statistical comparability protocol

This is a separately declared protocol; the original strict identical-population-size gate and its 33-row history remain unchanged. The target remains held-out natural-log validation loss. Use the eight rows with archived final logs matching the author's notebook, after excluding 146m14b14b because its explicit 11.3B script budget conflicts materially with the reported 14B. Selection is by source availability and budget conflict, not loss or holdout residual. Preserve N >= 2e9 as holdout.

Require the same GPT2BPETokenizer, vocabulary/merges paths, C4 validation dataset prefix, full validation split and loss definition in the matching logs. Different evaluation sample budgets are admitted as estimates of the same held-out C4 population; they do not establish identical samples or independent errors. Primary fit: equal point weights, official reported N/D coordinates, fixed exponent grid 0.10..0.60 by 0.05, nonnegative E and positive A/B as the existing public fitter. If no admissible fit exists, retain the failure and separately report a nonnegative-boundary diagnostic, not a fabricated interior optimum.

Prespecified sensitivity: (1) author Appendix S shape-derived N estimates, (2) available launch-budget D in place of reported labels, expressly not a measured achieved-token counter, (3) both coordinate changes, (4) fixed wider exponent grid 0.05..0.80 by 0.05. These assess coordinate/model uncertainty and are not grounds to replace the primary fit according to held-out results. Evaluation variance is unknown; do not invent confidence intervals or inverse-variance weights from token counts. Same-model checkpoints and evaluation-prefix overlap imply correlation. The small source-verifiable subset does not reproduce the full paper's fitted law.
