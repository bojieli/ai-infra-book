GroqRack™ Compute Cluster.
The backbone of low latency, large-scale deployments.

For data center deployments, GroqRack provides an extensible accelerator network. Combining
the power of an eight GroqNode™ set, GroqRack features up to 64 interconnected chips. The result
is a deterministic network with an end-to-end latency of only 1.6µs for a single rack, ideal for
massive workloads and designed to scale out to an entire data center.
