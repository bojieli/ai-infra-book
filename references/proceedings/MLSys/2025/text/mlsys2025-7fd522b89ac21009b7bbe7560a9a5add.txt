                                                       A BSTRACT
     In recent years, collaborative learning (CL) has emerged as a promising approach for machine learning (ML) and
     data science across distributed edge devices. As the deployment of CL jobs increases, they inevitably contend for
     limited resources. However, efficient resource scheduling in this context is challenging because of the ephemeral
     nature and resource heterogeneity of devices, coupled with the overlapping resource requirements of diverse CL
     jobs. Existing resource managers often assign devices to CL jobs randomly for simplicity and scalability, but this
     approach compromises job efficiency.
     In this paper, we present Venn, a CL resource manager that efficiently schedules ephemeral, heterogeneous devices
     among multiple CL jobs to reduce the average job completion time (JCT). Venn formulates the Intersection
     Resource Scheduling (IRS) problem to identify complex resource contention among multiple CL jobs. It then
     proposes a contention-aware scheduling heuristic to minimize the average scheduling delay. Furthermore, it
     proposes a resource-aware device-to-job matching heuristic to optimize response collection time by mitigating
     stragglers. Our evaluation shows that, compared to the state-of-the-art CL resource managers, Venn improves the
     average JCT by up to 1.88⇥. The code is available at https://github.com/SymbioticLab/Venn.
