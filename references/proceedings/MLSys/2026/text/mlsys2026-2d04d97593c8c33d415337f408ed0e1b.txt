B.2 Ternary quantization baseline on PTB-XL
Setup. To contextualize the extreme size regime, we imple-
ment a ternary-weight baseline following standard ternary
quantization (Li et al., 2016; Zhu et al., 2017). We evaluate
on PTB-XL with a fold split (folds 1–8 train, 9 validation,
10 test). Both H YPERT INY PW and ternary are trained for
20 epochs with Adam (lr = 10−3 ), batch size 32, weight
decay 10−5 , and ReduceLROnPlateau (factor 0.5, patience
3). To mitigate imbalance we use weighted cross-entropy
(class weights [1.16, 0.88]).
Size accounting. Ternary weights use 2 bits/weight plus
learned per-output-channel scale factors (one FP32 scale
per output channel), yielding 6.70 kB packed size in our
implementation.
