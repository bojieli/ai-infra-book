Hello!
I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science, University of
                        Central Florida (UCF). I am leading the Compiler Architecture group at UCF. I
                        generally develop 
                        architecture/compiler co-design solutions to improve
                        performance, reduce hardware complexity, and address
                        reliability/security problems.
I received my Ph.D degree from
Purdue University
under the guidance of
Prof. Changhee Jung
in 2022 and BS/MS from Kentucky State University.
        Prior to joining in UCF, I worked at
ARM Research
in 2020 with
Prof. Fredrik Kjolstad
in Stanford and
COMMIT
group in MIT.
	I also worked for NASA EpSCoR under
Prof. Alexandre Martin
at University of Kentucky (2017).
        I was a research engineer at
LG Electronics
Car Laboratory (2012-2015). I am an IEEE Senior Member, and I was honored to receive an ACM SIGBED Paul Caspi Memorial Dissertation Award (2023).
