Farid Samandi

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Stony Brook University, working with Prof. Mike Ferdman in COMPAS lab. My research broadly focuses on Computer Architecture. I am particularly interested in processor micro-architecture, and memory systems. I got my Masters and Bachelors degrees in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology and Amirkabir University of Technology, respectively [CV, Google Scholar].
