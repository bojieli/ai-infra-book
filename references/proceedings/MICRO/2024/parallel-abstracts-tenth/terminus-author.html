CAPTCHA
I am a Professor at MIT's Electrical Engineering and Computer Science Department and a member of the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. I work in computer architecture and computer systems. My current research focuses on large-scale multicores with hundreds to thousands of cores, scalable and efficient memory hierarchies, architectures with quality-of-service guarantees, and scalable runtimes and schedulers.

Before joining MIT in September 2012, I earned a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University, where I worked with Professor Christos Kozyrakis. I have also received an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Stanford (2009) and a B.S. in Telecommunications Engineering from the Technical University of Madrid, UPM (2007).
