About Me

I am currently with the Memory System Research Group at SK hynix America. My research interests include computer architecture, memory systems, Compute Express Link (CXL), near-memory acceleration, heterogeneous computing, and systems for machine learning.

I received my Ph.D. in computer science from the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), advised by Prof. Nam Sung Kim. I was affiliated with the FAST research group and Coordinated Science Lab.

Before joining UIUC, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering and Finance from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I have worked as a visiting researcher at Meta and as a research intern at Luminous Computing and Intel Labs.
