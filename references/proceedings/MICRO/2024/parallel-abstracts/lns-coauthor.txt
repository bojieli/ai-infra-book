About Me
I'm Andrew Gui — an ECE undergrad at the University of Washington. I split my time between research at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, where I work on SensorFish technology, and shipping software — most recently MyFitnessTech, an iOS app built with Flutter and Firebase. I also co-authored AI hardware research published at MICRO 2024.
I founded Fitness Tech at UW and box with the Husky Boxing Team. When I'm not building something, I'm training, wakeboarding, skiing, or finding the next thing worth learning.

SipSync
Prototype
Smart bottle — Fitness Tech hardware
A water bottle that weighs every sip. A load cell reads the bottle and an IMU catches the tilt, but a sip
                only counts once the water settles and the weight actually drops — so bumps and set-downs don't
                register. A XIAO ESP32-C3 streams it over BLE into MyFitnessTech as live hydration: sips, ounces drank,
                ounces left.
