    About Tony and IntelliArch Lab

    BACKGROUND

    The PI: Dr. Tony Geng is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Departments of ECE and CS at Rice University, where he directs the IntelliArch (Intelligent Architecture) Lab. He also holds an adjunct faculty appointment at the University of Rochester. Before entering academia, Dr. Geng worked in the Physical & Computational Sciences Directorate at the U.S. Department of Energy’s Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL). His research sits at the intersection of computer architecture and systems, generative AI, and AI for scientific exploration.​ ​

    ​

    The Lab: Dr. Geng and his students have published over 100 papers in top-tier conferences and journals across AI and computer architecture, including ISCA, MICRO, HPCA, OSDI, ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, AAAI, DAC, SC, TPDS, TC, and TIP. The IntelliArch Lab is equipped with extensive computing resources, including in-house servers powered by NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs, as well as access to over 100,000 GPU node hours annually on leading supercomputing and cloud platforms. The lab maintains long-term collaborations with industry leaders such as Meta and Microsoft through formal research partnerships and contracts. Its research is strongly supported by a diverse portfolio of funding sources, including major federal agencies such as DARPA, DOE, and NSF, as well as industry sponsors such as Meta, Microsoft, and AMD.

    Projects

    Projects

    Efficient

    Generative AI

    Pioneering next-generation GenAI through innovative architecture-algorithm co-design, we aim to jointly enhance efficiency, security, and reasoning across all stages of GenAI with a focus on LLMs & Diffusion Models and their multi-agent systems. 

    AI for Broadly-Defined Science

    Recognizing the soaring expressivity of GenAI, we are pioneering its applications in scientific exploration. Current interests include developing domain LLMs & diffusion models and their multi-agent systems for complex system analysis.

    Brain-Inspired Dynamical-System AI

    “Using Nature as a Low-Power Supercomputer” is no longer a dream! We’re creating brain-inspired AI processors that tap into nature’s dynamical systems to power the next wave of generative intelligence.
