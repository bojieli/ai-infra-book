SipSync Prototype

Smart bottle — Fitness Tech hardware

A water bottle that weighs every sip. A load cell reads the bottle and an IMU catches the tilt, but a sip only counts once the water settles and the weight actually drops — so bumps and set-downs don't register. A XIAO ESP32-C3 streams it over BLE into MyFitnessTech as live hydration: sips, ounces drank, ounces left.

ESP32-C3 BLE HX711 Load Cell MPU-6050 NeoPixel 3D Printing
  • ~12 h on a 400 mAh LiPo after a firmware power pass
  • Custom carrier PCB in KiCad — in progress
