About Me

I am an AI chip architect at ByteDance. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University (THU), where I was advised by associate Prof. Kaisheng Ma and worked closely with Prof. Dong Xiang and Prof. Torsten Hoefler. I received my B.S. degree in Information Engineering with Zhiyuan honor degree and second bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at Shanghai Jiaotong University (SJTU).

Work Scope (Research Interests)

  • AI Chip Architecture: chip-level SPEC/Arch definition & tuning, preliminary architecture exploration, hardware/software co-design, performance modeling, and cross-functional alignment for PPA/RTL delivery
  • AI Infrastructure: rack/cluster-level SPEC/Arch definition & tuning, AI scale-up/out network, communication library, distributed training/inference, performance/TCO optimization
