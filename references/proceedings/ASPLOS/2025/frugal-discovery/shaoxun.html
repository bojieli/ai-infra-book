About Me

I am currently working in the WeLM team at Tencent, where I am advancing systems for LLM training, inference, and reinforcement learning. I received my Ph.D. from Tsinghua University in 2026, supervised by Prof. Youyou Lu and Prof. Jiwu Shu.

My research focuses on system optimizations for LLMs, particularly on infrastructure scaling, resource efficiency, and full-stack co-design. If you are interested in democratizing access to state-of-the-art AI for billions of users and conducting cutting-edge research, join us.
