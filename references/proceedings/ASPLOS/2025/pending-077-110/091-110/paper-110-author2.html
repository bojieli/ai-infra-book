some facts about my name

In Chinese, my name writes as , corresponding to `Wáng Wén Yǚ` in Pinyin (note that the family name comes before the given name). As indicated by my given name, my parents wish that I could be a knowledgeable person.

Many English speakers would get confused by its pronunciation. In fact, the first two characters can be almost correctly read as `won` and `when`. The third character is somewhat tricky that it is often mistakenly pronounced as `you`, since the sound of `ü` in Pinyin does not exist in English. Nevertheless, there is a simple fix for this problem: try making the English `ee` sound and then slowly round the lips (thanks to ChinesePronounciationWiki).
