Welcome to the PICTure Research Group!

PICTure (Programming Systems and Intelligent Computing for Future) is a research group at the Elmore Family School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Purdue University, conducting research in the area of compiler technology and intelligent programming systems, with the emphasis on heterogeneous parallel computing, memory performance, data-intensive computing, high-performance machine learning, and real-time AI.

The group is led by Dr. Xipeng Shen.
