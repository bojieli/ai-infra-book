Abhishek Vijaya Kumar

I like to design and build ML systems.
I graduated with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Cornell University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Rachee Singh. I am currently working on RL infrastructure for post-training LLMs. My recent work in this space looks at reducing overheads of calling external tools during RL post-training and inference. During my PhD, I have also worked on exciting projects related to communication optimization for distributed machine learning. My work covers both electrical interconnects like Nvlinks and emerging silicon photonics interconnects like this.
Before Cornell, I spent two amazing years in the Systems group at Microsoft Research India, advised by Muthian Sivathanu and Saikat Guha. I received my undergraduate degree from PES University, Bengaluru.
