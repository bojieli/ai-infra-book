   Step 1: Taint Initialization. We begin the refinement
process with a coarse-grained taint scheme as the initial
configuration. Specifically, we assign a single taint bit for
the entire module, which tracks whether any of the registers
in the module hold tainted values, and apply the naive taint
logic using no dynamic values. This scheme represents the
most coarse-grained tracking capability and introduces the
lowest taint propagation overhead. We also refer to it as a
blackboxing taint scheme.

   Step 2: Model Checking and Counterexample Valida-
