Cast ¶ Cast from float 8 to float 16 (or E5M10), bfloat16 (or E8M7), float32 (or E8M23) is easier.
The cast is exact. The conversion does not necessarily preserve the sign for
specific values such as -0 or -NaN . Cast to float 8 consists in finding the closest float 8
to the original float 32 value. It is usually done by shifting
and truncating. The conversion may with saturation, every value out of range
becomes the highest available value. Next table summarizes
