Brief Biography: Kun Wang received two Ph.D. degrees in computer science from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Nanjing, China, in 2009, and from the University of Aizu, Aizuwakamatsu, Japan, in 2018. He was a Post-Doctoral Fellow in UCLA, USA from 2013 to 2014. He was a Research Fellow in the Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong, from 2017 to 2018, and was a Senior Research Professor in UCLA, from 2018 to 2021. He is currently a Professor in School of Microelectronics, Fudan University, China. His current research interests are mainly in the area of AI Hardware Acceleration, FPGA and EDA Compiler. He is the recipient of IEEE TCGCC Outstanding Young Researcher Award 2021, the Best Paper Award/Candidate at ACM FPGA 2020, ACM CHI 2020, ACM MobiSys 2020, ACM SenSys 2019, IEEE ISJ (IEEE Systems Journal) 2019, IEEE TCBD 2019, CBD 2019, IEEE TCGCC 2018, and IEEE GLOBECOM 2016. He serves/served as an Editor of IEEE Transactions on Green Communications and Networking, IEEE Systems Journal, Journal of Network and Computer Applications, and a Guest Editor of IEEE Network, Future Generation Computer Systems, and Peer-to-Peer Networking and Applications.
