• Attention. The entire forward pass of attention mecha-

• MoE routing. In Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models, the

  normalizes the gating scores (involving max and sum re-

  based on the absolute maximum value (an abs-max reduc-
  tion). The quantized FP8 tensors are then used in GEMM
  operations, yielding a Quant + GEMM cascade.

          # step 1. store previous result
          pmax_prev [ qs ] = pmax [ qs ]
          # step 3. perform reduction
          pmax [ qs ] = max ( pmax [ qs ] , P [ qs , kvs ])

          # reduction3 : sum ( exp ( P - pmax ) )
          # step 1. store previous result
          psum_prev [ qs ] = psum [ qs ]

          # step 3. perform reduction
          psum [ qs ] += exp ( P [ qs , kvs ] - pmax [ qs ])
