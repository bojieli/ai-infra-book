ISCA 2024
The 51
th
International Symposium on Computer Architecture
June 29–July 3, 2024
Buenos Aires, Argentina
The historic ISCA 2024 is now over!
It was such an honor to host you all in the beautiful Buenos Aires. For this
inaugural ISCA edition in Latin America
, we received 430 participants
            from more than 30 countries. The conference had a rich and diverse technical program including 83 technical papers over 19 sessions.
ISCA 2024 also showcased the rich Argentinian culture, with a fantastic tango show and gala dinner at Tango Porteño, one of Argentina's
            most renowned tango venues.
If you would like to share additional photos, feel free to reach out to
Augusto and Esteban
and/or post it in your social media profiles with tag #ISCACONF2024
See you all in
Tokyo
!
